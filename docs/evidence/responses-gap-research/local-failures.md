## Local routing facts

- src/server/app.ts:130–145 handles all /v1/* through forward(); local app-level errors use JSON {error:{...}} (app.ts:26–40).
- src/server/upstream.ts:89–103 adapts only minimal-mode POST /v1/responses. Other lifecycle endpoints, including /responses/compact, /responses/input_tokens, conversations, retrieve, and delete pass through.
- src/server/upstream.ts:149–155 returns upstream status/body unchanged outside the model and Responses aggregation paths. Thus the saved HTML 403 captures came through the upstream response path; their precise remote component is unknown.
- src/server/response-adapter.ts:39–44 is the confirmed local store:true gate. Its loose schema and spread at lines 7–13, 31–57 retain extra Responses fields, so parameter/tool 400s were not produced by a local field allowlist.
